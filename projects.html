Ember - In Progress 🚧

A quiet place to put down things you want to come back to later: links, products, videos, notes, places.

A native iOS app in SwiftUI and SwiftData, with no backend, no accounts and no third-party dependencies. Saving happens from the iOS share sheet: Safari → Share → Ember → Save puts you back in Safari in about a second, because the share extension writes and exits without ever touching the network. Enrichment runs afterwards inside the app, reading JSON-LD and Open Graph to fill in the title, thumbnail and source in place. Anything saved can be opened, kept, or let go with a four-second undo.

Swift SwiftUI SwiftData iOS Share Extension

Momentum - In Progress 🚧

A project and task manager with an AI assistant that turns a sentence into changes to your work.

A project and task manager built on Next.js and TypeScript with Prisma. The assistant is tool-calling over the Claude API: it creates, edits, deletes and bulk-reschedules tasks, and asks for clarification rather than guessing. A whole sentence is recorded as a single undo no matter how many things it changed, and token cost is metered per model. Access is enforced on the server rather than in the interface: the admin area returns a 404 to anyone without the role instead of just hiding the button. Sessions are database rows, so removing someone signs them out on their next request instead of whenever a token would have expired.

TypeScript Next.js Prisma Claude API AI Assistant
