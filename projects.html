Pixelator

Game

Draw pixel art like it’s 1989.

Pixelator is a pixel art studio with a Game Boy soul. Pick a colour, drag across the grid, and watch your art appear one square at a time. No brushes, no layers, no learning curve. Just you and a canvas full of pixels.

Build a custom six-slot palette as you work, tint the background and grid lines to whatever suits your piece, and step backwards and forwards through your history whenever a stroke doesn’t land. When it’s finished, one tap saves your masterpiece straight to your device as a PNG.

And you won’t be working in silence. A built-in chiptune player keeps four tracks on rotation while MegaMan dances along beside your canvas, until you hit Erase All and he charges up and blasts the whole thing clean.

There’s also a hidden MegaMan somewhere on the screen. Find him and claim your 1-Up.

Features

  • Click or drag to paint smooth, gap-free strokes
  • Full touch support, built for phones and tablets
  • Unlimited colours with a custom six-slot palette
  • Adjustable background and grid colours
  • Undo and redo, with keyboard shortcuts
  • One-tap PNG export
  • Four-track chiptune player with volume control

Tech stack

  • CoreVanilla JavaScript (ES2020+), HTML5 and CSS3, no framework, no build step
  • LayoutCSS Grid and Flexbox, responsive from 320px to widescreen
  • CanvasDOM pixel grid with pointer hit-testing, so mouse and touch share one path
  • HistoryDiff-based undo/redo
  • Librarieshtml2canvas (lazy-loaded) for export, SweetAlert2 for dialogs
  • AssetsAnimated WebP, 321 KB first load
  • HostingGitHub Pages

Called Out

Game

Who’s most likely to… get called out? The ultimate “who’s most likely to” game.

Gather your crew, grab your phones, and find out what everyone really thinks. One person starts a room, everyone else joins by code or QR, no app install, no accounts. Each round, a “Who’s most likely to…” question pops up on every screen and you all vote on the friend who fits best. Watch the votes reveal live, crown the round’s winner, and at the end each player walks away with a personality title they definitely didn’t ask for.

~980 questions across 10 categories. Fully bilingual in English and Arabic (with right-to-left support), plus music, sound effects, and confetti for the winners. Add it to your home screen and it plays like a native app.

Fast. Chaotic. A little too honest. 🎤
