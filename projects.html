Ember

In progress

An iPhone app that saves links, photos and notes for later, and sorts them for you.

Ember is for the things you come across but have no time for yet, like an article, a product or an idea. Save them from any app through the Share menu, and Ember adds a title and picture, sorts each into a category such as Food or Travel, and lets you search them all. It is built with Swift, SwiftUI and SwiftData, Apple's own tools for iPhone apps, and keeps everything on your phone with no account needed.

Momentum

In progress

A project and task planner whose AI assistant updates your plan from one sentence you type.

Momentum is a web app for people and teams juggling several projects, made to cut time spent updating tasks and writing status reports. You can see one plan as a list, task board, timeline or calendar, ask the AI assistant in plain words to reschedule work, and get a weekly progress update drafted for you. Built with Next.js and TypeScript, it stores data in a Postgres database on Neon, is hosted on Vercel and uses Claude, Anthropic's AI model.

Swift Comprehension Practice

A free website for practicing the Saville Swift aptitude tests used in many hiring processes.

It covers Swift Comprehension, for operational and admin roles, and Swift Executive, for senior roles, with questions on reading, numbers, spotting errors and shape puzzles. Practice one section or a full mock exam, then see your score, weak spots and every answer explained. Built with JavaScript, HTML and CSS, it is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Unmasked

In progress

A conversation card game that helps groups get past small talk and know each other better.

Unmasked is a conversation game for friends, couples, families or new teams, played on one shared phone in English or Saudi Arabic. Players take turns drawing question cards from three decks, from light icebreakers to deeper questions, pick topics such as travel, work or family, and can choose to mix in AI-written questions. Built with Next.js and TypeScript, it saves games in a Supabase database, is hosted on Vercel and uses Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to write those questions.

TypeScript Next.js Supabase Vercel Claude Web App
